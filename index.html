TESTNET
Read before trading. This places real orders on your exchange account when you tap Analyze & Trade. It only acts when you open the app and tap the button — it does not watch the market on its own. Stop-loss and take-profit are placed on the exchange so your position stays protected even after you close this page. Backtests and past results never guarantee future profit. Start on testnet.

Connection

Live trading
Off = Binance Testnet (fake money). On = your real account.
Keys are saved only in this browser, on this phone, in this app's own storage. They are never sent anywhere except directly to Binance's servers to place your orders. Get free testnet keys at testnet.binance.vision (sign in with GitHub).
Risk settings
Stop-loss = 1 ATR away. Take-profit = 2 ATR away. These settings are saved with your keys.

What this does & doesn't do

• Long only on spot — no shorting, no leverage, no margin.
• Fires only when you tap the button, not continuously in the background.
• Stop-loss and take-profit are real exchange orders, so they work even with the app closed.
• A daily loss cap and a drawdown kill switch block new trades and flatten your position.
• If your browser or network blocks Binance's API (CORS), live order calls will show an error here — testnet is the way to check that first.
